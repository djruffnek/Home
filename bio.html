About Ruffnek

Open Format Video DJ & Remixer

Ruffnek has been rocking crowds for 4 decades, as both a solo artist & as part of the Funk-infused band Slapbak.

Traveling on tour & sharing the stage with artists such as Snoop Dogg, Xzibit, Digital Underground, The Luniz, Arrested Development, K-Ci & Jojo, Earth Wind & Fire, Parliament/Funkadelic, The Time, The Gap Band, The Dazz Band, Slave, Zapp, Ohio Players, Living Colour, Brides Of Funkenstein and many more - playing in venues ranging from small night clubs to concert venues & performing on stages with crowds varying from 2,000 to 50,000 attendees.

Featured remixes

You can find Ruffnek’s remixes featured on remix services such as: Direct Music Service (DMS), TailoredTrax, YourRemix, Culture Shock, Mixshow Ingredients, Smash, Deejay Essentials, Crooklyn Clan & more.
